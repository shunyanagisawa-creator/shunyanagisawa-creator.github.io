Japan's Yen Swaps Go Global

Risk.net · 16 December 2025 · Sponsored by JPX/JSCC · Quoted in

Analysis of the landmark opening of JSCC to US investors following the CFTC No-Action Letter (September 12, 2025). Shun Yanagisawa is quoted on the strategic significance:

  • Citi's long-standing advocacy for open and accessible markets, and support for the expansion of client choice in clearing for US investors
  • Citi's role in clearing the first US client trade on JSCC — the result of years of preparation and close coordination with global clients
  • Strategic priority of a holistic and agnostic market access platform spanning futures, swaps, cash and repo across the entire yen rates ecosystem
  • Context: JSCC's structural dominance — DV01 of USD 1,481 million monthly (2025), 86% market share in tenors beyond 10 years

FOW

Japan's Rates Market Sees Liquidity Boost on Policy Shift

Futures & Options World · 9 April 2026 · Karry Lai · Interview

In-depth analysis on the structural transformation of Japan's yen rates market, including:

  • BOJ policy normalisation driving yen swap clearing volumes up 8× since 2021 — JPY 229 trillion daily average in 2025, with a record single-day volume of JPY 146 trillion (Dec 4, 2025)
  • CFTC No-Action Letter (September 2025) enabling US hedge funds — Citadel, Millennium, ExodusPoint et al. — to clear JPY IRS via JSCC; non-Japanese client base expanded to 65 firms
  • Non-resident participants growing from 44% to 65% of cleared notional (Q4 2025)
  • US SEC mandatory Treasury Repo clearing rules and the structural challenge for Japanese megabanks with US branches
  • JGB 10-year futures open interest exceeding JPY 170 trillion by end-2025; 20-year JGB futures open interest surpassing 40,000 lots (Jan 2026)

BOJ政策正常化を受けた円金利市場の構造転換、CFTCノーアクションレター（2025年9月）による米系ヘッジファンドのJSCC参入（Citadel・Millennium・ExodusPoint等65社）、米国SEC Treasuryレポマンデートが日系銀行に与える影響について詳述。円スワップ日次清算量8倍超（229兆円、2025年）、非居住者シェア65%（2025年Q4）などの具体的データを提供。
